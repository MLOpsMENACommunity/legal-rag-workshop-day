---

## Time-management rules

1. **Every workshop has a mid-point checkpoint.** If more than a third of the room has not reached it, switch to `solution/` and keep teaching. Do not wait.
2. **`solution/` is available from the start.** It is a safety net, not a reward: someone stuck in W1 should not lose the whole day.
3. **Anyone behind copies from their neighbour.** People work in pairs by default.
4. **Opening is exactly 20 minutes.** Broken setups get fixed during the break, not during teaching time.

---

## Logistics

- **Wi-Fi:** everyone will `docker pull` and `pip install` — the network is the biggest risk of the day. PREWORK solves this.
- **Screen:** code must be readable from the back row — large font, light theme.
- **USB stick:** carries the Docker images, models and PDF for anyone whose network fails.
